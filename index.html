Our Schengen Consultancy Services

Professional guidance and support at every stage of your Schengen application journey.

Eligibility Assessment

We review your travel plans, supporting documents, and personal circumstances to help determine your suitability for a Schengen application and identify the right path forward.

Document Review Guidance

Application Form Guidance

Appointment Guidance & Support

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about booking with Euro Path Travel.

How do I book a holiday or flight with Euro Path? +
Simply fill out our enquiry form on the Flights or Holiday Packages page, and one of our expert travel consultants will get back to you within 30 minutes with the best available deals. You can also call us directly on +44 7737 140585 for immediate assistance.
Can I pay for my holiday in instalments? +
Absolutely! We offer flexible payment plans to make your dream holiday more affordable. You can secure your booking with a deposit and pay the balance in manageable instalments before your departure date.
What happens if I need to change or cancel my booking? +
We understand plans can change. Our team will work with you to amend or cancel your booking. Cancellation terms vary depending on the airline and hotel, but we'll always find the best solution for you. We also recommend travel insurance for added protection.
Do you offer business and first-class flights? +
Yes, we specialise in finding the best deals across all cabin classes — Economy, Premium Economy, Business, and First Class. We have access to exclusive fares and can often find significant savings on premium cabin bookings.
How quickly will I receive my booking confirmation? +
Once your booking is confirmed and payment is processed, you'll receive a detailed confirmation email within 24 hours. This will include all your travel documents, itinerary, and emergency contact details.
